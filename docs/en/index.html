How the tutor works

Think for yourself first, then get more help step by step.

Every reply ends with exactly one next step. If the explanation is not enough, you type the next command word.

  1. erklär

    Topic and the equations you need. No example, no solution.

  2. tipp

    Guiding questions you ask yourself. The solution is held back.

  3. voll

    Intuitive and mathematical, with a worked example of a different problem.

  4. kurz

    Exam solution in German to compare, plus one remark.

Try it.

The same problem, four command words. Pick one and see how much help you get.

Shortened excerpts following the real reply structure; the problem comes from a test run on claude.ai. The full reply also contains the method, common pitfalls and a review card.

Ein Auto beschleunigt aus der Ruhe gleichmäßig in 8,0 s auf 24 m/s. Welche Strecke legt es dabei zurück?

Gegeben / Gesucht

v₀ = 0, v = 24 m/s, t = 8.0 s · wanted: distance s

Idee

The car gets faster by the same amount every second: uniformly accelerated motion. Imagine it drove the whole time at a fixed average speed; it would get just as far. This picture only holds as long as the acceleration stays constant.

Gleichungen

Velocity–time law and displacement–time law:

v=v0+at s=v0t+12at2

Verständnisfrage

Why may you use the average of the initial and final velocity here?

No solution. Work it out yourself, about 10 minutes; otherwise tipp.

Commands

Send a photo of the problem, optionally with a command word. !an tipp keeps a mode for all following problems, !aus ends it.

Research

Built on 23 studies from learning research.

The tutor’s teaching rules follow principles backed by experiments and seven meta-analyses. Every study is evaluated openly in the repository.

Kestin et al. 2025 · Scientific Reports 2×

That is how much Harvard students in introductory physics learned with an AI tutor built on learning principles: more than twice as much as in active in-person teaching, in 49 instead of 60 minutes. They also felt more engaged and motivated.

What matters is how it is built: with free AI access, around 1,000 learners did worse in a later test without help; with an AI tutor that had safeguards, they did not (Bastani et al. 2025, PNAS).

The tutor does both: it guides you step by step and gives the solution only on request.

  • 01 · Retrieval

    Recall it yourself instead of rereading

    Actively retrieving what you learned keeps it better after days than reading it again.

    Roediger & Karpicke 2006 · Adesope et al. 2017

    In the tutor: explanations end with a Verständnisfrage (comprehension question); the answer comes only on request.

  • 02 · Self-explanation

    Put the why into your own words

    Prompts to justify steps yourself clearly improve learning on average (g = 0.55), especially when they ask about concepts.

    Chi et al. 1989, 1994 · Bisra et al. 2018

    In the tutor: tipp asks guiding questions, at least one about the why.

  • 03 · Examples

    Worked through, but not your problem

    Fully worked examples improve maths performance (g = 0.43). They help beginners most; advanced learners need fewer of them.

    Sweller & Cooper 1985 · Kalyuga et al. 2003 · Barbieri et al. 2023

    In the tutor: voll works through a different problem.

  • 04 · Feedback

    Say exactly what is wrong

    Feedback with information works much better than praise or a bare right/wrong.

    Wisniewski, Zierer & Hattie 2020

    In the tutor: check names the first mistake with the reason.

  • 05 · Help on request

    AI as a supplement, not a replacement

    People who get explanations from AI understand a topic better than people who let it produce the solution.

    Lehmann et al. 2024/25 · Bastani et al. 2025

    In the tutor: help in steps from erklär to kurz.

  • 06 · Spaced practice

    Review with gaps in between

    The best gap between study sessions depends on how long you want to remember.

    Cepeda et al. 2006, 2008 · Dunlosky et al. 2013

    In the tutor: the review card plans “in 2–3 days and again after about a week”.

The studies support the learning principles the tutor is built on. All sources with study design and assessment (in German):learning research review and evidence update.

Setup

Upload once, then learn.

The tutor is not a program and not a service. It runs as a skill in your Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account; all you need is a browser.

Claude Skill recommended

  1. Download tutor-skill.zip. Do not unzip it.

  2. On claude.ai, turn on “Code execution and file creation” under Settings → Capabilities. Skills do not run without it.

  3. Under Customize → Skills, click “+” → “Create skill” → “Upload a skill” and choose the ZIP file. The skill “tutor” must be switched on.

  4. To test, type only this word in a new chat – the command card appears:

    tutor

  5. Then send a photo of the problem, optionally with a command word like tipp.

Menu names as in the Claude help; other interface languages may use different names. Team and Enterprise accounts: skills are enabled by the admins.

Claude Code: install as a plugin in one line (version 2.1.275 or later):

/plugin install tutor --marketplace Sae-l/tutor

ChatGPT · Codex · Gemini experimental

The same rules also run in ChatGPT, Codex and Gemini. All files are in release v0.3.0-rc.5.

Guide for ChatGPT, Codex and Gemini

Codex and ChatGPT Desktop: add the plugin source, then install “Tutor” in Codex with /plugins or in the plugin directory of ChatGPT Desktop.

codex plugin marketplace add Sae-l/tutor

ChatGPT on the web: workspace admins upload tutor-chatgpt.zip under Admin → Plugins → Upload plugin. Without the plugin, the project route with kern.md and system-prompt.md works. Setup and functional check (in German). The ChatGPT routes have not been accepted yet.

Gemini: in the Gemini app, under Settings → Skills → Create, upload tutor-skill.zip unchanged; call it with / or @. Requirements according to the Google help: 18+, “Keep Activity” on, uploading only on the web or in the Mac app. Not tested; it is open whether Gemini accepts the ZIP with the folder tutor/ at the top level.

Gemini Gem (being phased out): kern.md as instructions, system-prompt.md as knowledge. Gems end for personal accounts from November 2026, for Workspace in 2027.

Menu names may differ; formula rendering and field limits have not been tested there.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What kinds of problems is the tutor made for?

For exercise and exam problems in physics, maths, electrical engineering and engineering mechanics at university. You send a photo or the text of the problem, optionally with a command word like tipp.

What does the tutor cost?

Nothing. The tutor is free, ad-free and open source (MIT licence). You only need a Claude account; according to the Claude help, skills are available on all plans, including the free one.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes: you upload the skill once in the browser on claude.ai. After that you photograph problems in your phone’s browser and send them straight away. The Claude app has not been fully tested for this yet.

Can I add my course?

Yes, with a course pack: a Markdown file with the notation and syllabus of your course, e.g. kurse/mpg1.md. Upload it as project knowledge in a Claude project and study in that project.

Is the tutor ADHD-friendly?

It is designed for it: short sections, always the same order and, at the end, exactly one next step with a time estimate.

What is new in v0.3.0-rc.5?

Replies are more consistent: “Gegeben / Gesucht” is always a table, numbers use Latin digits in Arabic and Persian too, and in erklär the solutions come at the very end. Since v0.3.0-rc.4, explanations are available in Persian instead of Urdu ([fa]). Carried over from v0.3.0-rc.3: erklär, voll and thema now explain along a fixed arc: an opening, one picture, its limit, a core sentence and the bridge to the equation. tipp asks about the why and offers a different approach if you write “weiß nicht” (don’t know). The review card plans two dates, formula symbols appear in the form that suits the platform, and there are subject rules for electrical engineering, engineering mechanics and maths. Everything in the changelog.

The tutor does not behave as described.

Check that “Code execution and file creation” is on and that the skill “tutor” is active under Customize → Skills. Then open a new chat and type only tutor. If no command card appears, delete the skill and upload the ZIP file again.

The upload fails.

Upload the ZIP file unchanged. Unzipping and re-zipping it often loses the folder tutor/ at the top level; then Claude does not recognise the skill.

Formulas show up with dollar signs.

On claude.ai in the browser, formulas are rendered. If you see dollar signs, the platform does not display LaTeX; that is why the tutor writes formula symbols for ChatGPT in italics with subscripts (v₀).

How do I get a new version?

Download the new ZIP file, delete the old skill under Customize → Skills via “…” and upload the new file.
